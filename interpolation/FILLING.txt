BOUNDARY INTERPOLATION AND FILLING — PROPOSED CONTRACT, NOT A NEW API

Current capability
Polynomial evaluators supply boundary positions and derivatives. Existing
morphing.biological.polygon_field samples signed distance and even-odd
membership of closed polygon rings. Existing morphing.presentation.positions
and triangles clip a lattice field into triangle geometry. These source facts
do not qualify a new Interpolation boundary-to-fill integration.

Coordinate seam
polygon_field accepts vertex coordinates strictly inside (-1,1)^2 and ring
offsets measured in vertices, without a duplicated closing vertex. The last
vertex is connected to the first within each ring. At least three vertices and
nonzero polygon edges are required. A sampled interpolated curve therefore
needs an explicit closure/duplicate policy and sampling/error policy.
The square lattice maps to presentation output [0,extent]^2. Returning to the
original local frame uses 2*x/extent-1 and 2*y/extent-1. A consumer must declare
this transform; source coordinates and output positions are not interchangeable.

General graph policy
1. Declare an embedding/chart or explicit attached faces with owned boundary
   walks. A graph adjacency list, cycle basis or arbitrary 3D rim is insufficient.
2. Select filled face IDs, or explicitly request contour even-odd/nonzero winding.
   Exclude the unbounded face by default. Do not silently turn every cycle into
   a filled disk; neighboring cycles can share edges, enclose holes or overlap.
3. Distinguish boundary, internal seam and wire edges. A dangling wire makes no
   face. A bridge joining outer and inner loops does not erase the hole. Repeated
   bridge occurrences in a face walk are not independent hole contours.
4. Classify crossings as junctions or nonconnecting sheet/overpass occurrences.
   Planar face extraction splits actual crossings. Ambiguous tangencies and
   coincident edges need explicit ownership/predicates, not guessed topology.
5. Interior coordinates name a containing face/sheet and a role: sample, hard
   anchor/jet, soft control or topology vertex. A coordinate does not itself make
   a hole. Data in a missing face reject or require an explicit cell creation.
6. Choose an interior extension separately from selecting the filled region.
   Constant planar color, scalar interpolation and a nonplanar spanning surface
   are different requests. The latter needs a patch/chart/energy rule; the rim
   alone cannot choose a unique surface. Neighboring faces reuse an owned C0
   trace and separately select crease/smooth transported-jet laws.
7. Track ideal boundary, sampled polygon, selected cells, triangle mesh and
   rendered output separately. Topology edits invalidate affected face ownership
   and tessellation. Sampling is not proof of continuous-curve topology.

Implementation status
No general arrangement/DCEL face extractor, face-policy validator, curved-loop
sampling adapter, triangulation/error certificate or 3D spanning solver has been
added by this contract. The new graph module is source-only edge interpolation;
it does not automatically supply faces or fills. Existing numerical native PASS
does not qualify these proposed additions or WASM/render integration.
