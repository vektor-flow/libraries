execstart-capture.json preserve exact command argv, exit, stdout, stderr and UTC.
introspection-capture.json independently reports ExecStart a(sasbttttuii).

ignore_failure,start_realtime,start_monotonic,exit_realtime,exit_monotonic,pid,

fields. Outer then indexed service and inner_result despite attach not persisting.
