Exact numeric algorithm and source argument
from_token never constructs a floating-point number. It reads a sign, exact
integer decimal coefficient as digits and decimal power; irrelevant leading
zeros are removed. Exponents saturate beyond token byte length+2000, where even
all mantissa/fraction digits cannot bring a nonzero value into a finite rounding
boundary. Zero remains zero regardless of exponent. Grammar is validated first.
Positive finite IEEE magnitude ranks range 0..9218868437227405311. For rank r,
exponent=floor(r/2^52), significand=r mod 2^52 (+2^52 for normals), dyadic power
is -1074 for subnormals and exponent-1075 for normals. The midpoint ABOVE r is
exactly (2*significand+1)*2^(power-1), including binade transitions. Rank zero's
upper midpoint is 2^-1075. The maximum's upper midpoint is the overflow boundary.
Repeated integer digit multiplication by 2 or 5 represents that dyadic exactly
as a decimal integer times 10^power. Products are <=49; coefficient<=2^54-1;
ranks and midpoint-search arithmetic fit signed int64. Decimal comparison first
compares digit-length+power, then digits padded on the right with zeros.
inside includes ties exactly when rank is even (round-to-nearest/ties-to-even).
Binary search returns the first rank whose upper boundary includes the token.
The max odd-rank midpoint is excluded, rejecting overflow, not coercing it.
from_number obtains the exact positive rank from an existing binary64 by exact
power-of-two normalization, integral significand extraction and integer shifts
for subnormals. No decimal parse/format occurs. Rank and sign must match exactly.
No epsilon, truncation to decimal precision, common formatter, or map equality.
Zero signs canonicalize as in the existing json_numbers roundtrip specification;
this is numerical binary64 equality, not an added signed-zero bit contract.
Nonzero adjacent ranks remain distinct, including the two adjacent 0.1 values.
