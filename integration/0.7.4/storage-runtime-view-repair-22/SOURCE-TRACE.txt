WHY NOT JUST COVER /run?
The old command recursively binds host / into newroot. An overmount may make
inaccessible descendants hidden to path resolution, but cannot be advertised as
removing their mountinfo records. A --dir /run after that clone does not mask
anything. The requested stronger property is achieved by never copying host /
or its /run,/var,/sys subtrees into the final workload root.
