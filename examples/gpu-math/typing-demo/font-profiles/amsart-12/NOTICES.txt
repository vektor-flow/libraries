Native optical AMS profile: Computer Modern / AMS fonts, BBM METAFONT glyphs.
CM/AMS notices and SIL OFL are distributed in ../../AMS-FONTS-NOTICES.txt and ../../OFL.txt.
BBM copyright 1993 Gilles F. Robert; original source archive and README accompany this profile.
Glyph metrics, outlines and masks were prepared offline; no article or reference PDF was used to prepare these reusable assets.
Reusable 96dpi masks are prepared offline with MuPDF from individual native font programs (including BBM PK font templates), with no article/reference PDF input. MTSDF remains available at other zooms. MuPDF, pdfTeX and METAFONT are development tools, not runtime dependencies.
