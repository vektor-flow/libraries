Required geometry contract
Declare phi<0 in filled ink, n_out=grad(phi)/|grad(phi)| and n_in=-n_out.
Use the actual nonzero-winding material region: holes, overlapping contours,
ligatures/rules and unions can hide boundaries that must not emit twice. Source
and target fields retain native font/program/resource/layout identities.
Physical affine transforms matter: gradients transform with A^-T; nonuniform
stretch/shear does not preserve Euclidean distance by a single scale. Re-distance
in the chosen physical metric, or state the approximation/error. Corners, cusps,
medial axes, singular transforms and near-zero gradients need explicit handling.
Use a declared physical-width narrow band and bounded normal regularization.
Distance decreases toward a boundary; it is NOT itself temperature or pressure.

Coupling, not prescribed particle paths
A profile a(phi)*grad(phi)=grad(F(phi)) is a pure gradient and is removed by the
periodic Leray projection. Setting outward air velocities and projecting them
cannot be advertised as normal expansion. Geometry can supply a declared
surface mass flux, material momentum/heat flux or porous continuity source, with
balanced air-volume return and pressure boundary conditions. Outgoing attached
ink must debit its finite native reservoir; no ambient smoke creation. Target
inward flux/hazard must credit the FIRST receiver from actual captured stock;
later ordinary waves and matched native state interpolation remain separate.

The current probes are overdamped, one-way diagnostics. Their stored velocity
is terminal resolved air velocity, not a Brownian derivative. Merely assigning
an initial outward probe velocity has no effect on their current dx update.
An emission impulse therefore needs a qualified finite-response particle model
or conservative momentum/heat coupling into shared air. Finite-tau drag is only
qualified for frozen sampled air. Coarse MAC cells cannot resolve thin glyph
stroke jets without a declared conservative subgrid boundary treatment.
