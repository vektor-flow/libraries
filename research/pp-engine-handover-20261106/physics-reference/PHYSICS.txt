PHYSICAL SMOKE REFERENCE AND VKF IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

Scope and ownership
This is a numerical research reference, not an alternative Product renderer or
an installed VKF fluid backend. Platonic Play must not import this Python solver.
VKF owns the eventual client solver, native glyph capture, plots, material
rendering, state interpolation and GPU execution. The current published smoke
backend has NOT been replaced by this reference. The prescribed analytic-vortex
experiment was superseded before public integration.

The physical model
A three-dimensional staggered MAC grid carries air velocity u, pressure p,
temperature deviation T, and three nonnegative pigment concentrations c_k.
The example parameters are NONDIMENSIONAL, not calibrated properties of actual
ink or air. For SI use, declare L_ref, t_ref, rho_ref and DeltaT_ref: viscosity
scales as L_ref^2/t_ref, force as L_ref/t_ref^2, volume flow as L_ref^3/t_ref.

In constant-reference-density conservative form:
  div u = s_in - s_out
  du/dt = -div(u tensor u) - grad p + div(2 nu_eff S) + b
          + s_in u_in - s_out u
  dc_k/dt = -div(c_k u) + div(D_eff grad c_k) - s_out c_k
  d captured_k/dt = integral(s_out c_k dV)
  dT/dt = -div(T u) + div(alpha_eff grad T) - (cooling+s_out)T.
Here u_in=0 and incoming air has no pigment and ambient temperature. Mean
buoyancy is included in the hydrostatic reference; only temperature/loading
contrasts force the periodic velocity field. There are no ambient smoke sources.

S is symmetric strain. nu_eff = nu + (Cs Delta)^2 sqrt(2 S:S), with nonnegative
Smagorinsky eddy viscosity. The staggered discretization filters squared shear
before taking its norm, so grid-scale opposing shears do not incorrectly cancel.
D_eff = D_molecular + nu_t/Sc_t, and alpha_eff similarly uses Pr_t.
The example Cs=.12, Sc_t=.7 and Pr_t=.7 are declared closure choices. This is a
coarse large-eddy model, NOT a DNS or an engineering validation of turbulence.
No prescribed swirl paths, independent decorative vortices or vorticity-
confinement energy injection replace Navier-Stokes evolution. Initial smoothed
random velocity perturbations and heat/source conditions are recorded by seed.

Particle interaction and diffusion
Fine visual/material probes share the SAME solved air field; they are not
independently animated. Pressure, viscous stress, advection and buoyancy couple
the flow. The supported regime is dilute, small-Stokes-number aerosol/tracers.
There are no hard-sphere contacts or a qualified finite-inertia two-way drag model.
The optional finite-response drag helper is exact only for frozen sampled air.

For a spatially variable diffusivity K, the overdamped Ito probe equation is
  dX = [u(X,t) + grad K(X,t)] dt + sqrt(2K(X,t)) dW.
The grad K drift is essential: its Fokker-Planck equation is the SAME Fickian
advection/diffusion equation, rather than an artificial pile-up where K is small.
Random increments model only unresolved/molecular diffusion. Resolved motion and
vortices come from the nonlinear 3D fluid solver. Stored probe velocity is terminal
RESOLVED AIR VELOCITY u(X_end), not the undefined instantaneous derivative of a
Brownian path and not an independent inertial momentum.

Continuum concentrations and fine probes are TWO discretizations of the same
material. NEVER add their masses together. The continuum supplies mean loading;
probes are dilute diagnostics of that field. Check their uptake/distribution
agreement with sampling confidence AND discretization error. For production ink
carriers, choose one authoritative material ledger and qualify any particle/grid
transfer; do not silently relabel these probes as an exact two-way particle fluid.

A collector, not a magical endpoint
Text attraction is an explicit controlled porous collector, not a spontaneous
property of Navier-Stokes. Nonnegative collector/supply masks each integrate to1:
  s_out = Q(t) W_collector, s_in = Q(t) W_return.
The separate pigment-free return-air supply balances removed AIR volume. Pressure
then enforces div u=s; divergence is zero in bulk, and prescribed at porous ports.
This is an OPEN, periodically bounded reference volume, not a sealed box. Pump
pressure work and port momentum exchange must be counted before claiming energy
conservation. This implementation qualifies periodic boundaries only.

Native VKF must supply actual glyph/front masks and source ink. The solver accepts
fields, not handcrafted fonts or triangles. Only the FIRST new creation receives
the outgoing material; later rows reveal normally. Captured mass/flux must drive
materialization. Do not prescribe a deadline and then teleport or discard smoke.
Finite-rate continuum uptake has an exponential tail: retain every residual mass.
Fine probes use a sampled exponential optical-depth threshold and accumulated
physical capture hazard, so their actual absorption events debit gas and credit
the receiver. Preserve these thresholds/exposures across a view change.

Source pigment composition is conserved by transport. A requested fade toward a
new authored text colour is a separate native rendering/state-interpolation
attribute, not a claim that fluid dynamics chemically changes the pigment.
The model starts with released aerosol; latent heat, boiling and ink chemistry
are NOT solved. Glyph-to-gas release must be an explicit conservative source
transfer from the native attached-material reservoir, with declared heat/momentum.

Numerical method and boundary movement
Velocity lives on faces, scalars at cell centres. The same staggered divergence
D and gradient G define the pressure operator DG. Pressure projection and all
stencils are compatible. Momentum fluxes are conservative; symmetric viscous
stress is dissipative; pigments use conservative limited MUSCL fluxes.

For nonstationary ports, use a gradient lift L with D L=s and u=w+L. Since L is a
gradient, the incompressible projection annihilates dL/dt, so evolve divergence-free
w with the projected momentum RHS. Reconstruct actual u at EVERY SSPRK3 stage
time t, t+dt, t+dt/2, including the convexly combined stages. Final projection alone
cannot repair inconsistent intermediate transport. A quintic C2 collector ramp
and convex C2 spatial-mask transitions preserve volume balance and avoid resets.
For a basis L=-G pL, physical stage pressure is p_rhs + d(Q pL)/dt, including the
spatial-mask blending derivative. The reference checks every stage's constraint,
positivity and transport bound, and final CFL too. Failed trial steps do not
mutate accepted state. Adaptive CFL subdivisions occur only inside fixed ticks.

Observation cadence cannot drive the integration schedule. advance_to(t) uses an
absolute observation clock (nanosecond input quantization) and integer fixed ticks.
A partially observed tick is not cut into a new physical step. Native rendering
may interpolate accepted frames separately; it must not feed that interpolation
back into physics. A view change or hold never resets or pauses the fluid clock.

Snapshots and replay
Persist the complete velocity/pressure/temperature/species state, deposited mass,
fixed tick/substep clock, source/control laws, normalized masks, pressure-lift
bases, active mask-transition state and random/probe state. Probe position,
resolved velocity, weight/species, alive mask, capture exposure/threshold and RNG
state all matter. Do not renormalize already-normalized snapshots on restoration.
Exact replay is qualified on the same CPU backend; GPU f32 comparisons need an
explicit measured tolerance and backend identity, not an inherited f64 claim.

Required native port gates
Run the supplied physics tests and independent falsification cases before a full
movie. Compare GPU/native Poisson residuals using D/G above; do not count fixed
Jacobi iterations as convergence. The current075 compiled-field ABI supports
persistent/ping-pong buffers and gather stencils. A GPU FFT, float atomics, particle
scatter, global reductions and a fluid solver are NOT automatically available.
Qualify the required implementation or expose the capability gap. Matrix-free
multigrid/PCG is acceptable if its residual/error criteria pass. Keep compute and
memory budgets, cancellation, fixed solver clock and immutable source provenance.

Current limitations
Periodic reference boundary; no qualified solid/open-wall GPU boundary backend.
Nondimensional demonstration parameters; no calibrated material/air-property fit.
Dilute probes, not a validated dense/collisional or finite-inertia two-way solver.
Continuum residual stays live; no exact zero-gas guarantee at a finite deadline.
Actual native glyph-front/colour coupling and client GPU implementation are the
next implementation work; the numerical reference alone does not establish them.
